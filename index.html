Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data shared with anyone?

Never. ExWise stores all data locally on your device using LocalStorage. It never leaves your browser unless you enable the optional cloud backup.

Can I export my data?

Yes! You can export all your records in PDF, JSON, or Excel formats anytime via the Settings menu for safe keeping.
